
CUSTOM.ZIP -> CUSTOM.TXT

Once you have registered, you can extract these files to your OLMS directory
or your language directory and edit them into anything you wish, in order to 
customize your display/keys.

Any files that are needed, but not present will be loaded from the SCREENS.DAT
file.  Please note, that even if you replace all the screens, SCREENS.DAT must 
still be present in your OLMS directory.

The *.OPT files are for redefining the keys that corrospond the -MAIN, -MAIN2
and -PREF ASC/ANS/RIP screens.  The KEYWORD.OPT reflects the three defined
keys for Global Keyword, Global Personal and Twit Personal.
